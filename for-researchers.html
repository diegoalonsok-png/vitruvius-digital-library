Vitruvius Digital Library · For Researchers

An independent, open-access digital research gateway focused on Vitruvius' De architectura, its textual transmission, manuscript witnesses, historical printed editions, translations, and critical study. Editorial and research materials are presented by Diego Kurilo. This website is not an institutional university repository and does not imply university endorsement.

Research resources

  • Historical bibliography and manuscript catalogue
  • Index codicum (manuscript witnesses)
  • Historical editions
  • Multilingual text reader: Latin, Spanish, French, English
  • Critical apparatus
  • Index locorum
  • Vitruvian lexicon
  • Passage comparison
  • Editorial methodology and limitations
  • Research and studies

Scope and citation

Readers should cite individual manuscripts, historical editions and modern publications according to the bibliographic information supplied in the relevant record. Do not attribute a DOI to any work unless that identifier has been registered and verified. Digitized primary-source descriptions and modern editorial interpretations should be distinguished when citing the collection.

External university research-guide listing

As of 2 November 2026, the University of Cambridge Faculty of Classics Library lists Vitruvius Digital Library among the open-access Latin resources in its public Classics LibGuide. This is a subject-guide link, not Cambridge sponsorship, scholarly peer review or institutional certification of the editorial text.

Open-access ancient-world resource listing

Charles E. Jones published VITRVVIVS DIGITAL LIBRARY on AWOL — The Ancient World Online, 1 November 2026. The specialist notice links to the library’s reader and research tools. It is external coverage, not validation of the textual criticism.
