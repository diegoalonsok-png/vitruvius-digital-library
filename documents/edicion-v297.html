Resumen / Abstract

An English-language research and reading edition presenting Vitruvius' De architectura with facing translation, commentary and studies of Roman architectural theory. V297 is not represented as a new recension of the Latin manuscript tradition.

Diego A. Kurilo's English-language study and Latin–English reading edition includes commentary, references and research apparatus. Cite the V297 version, relevant book-chapter-section, page and the DOI of the specific deposit.
