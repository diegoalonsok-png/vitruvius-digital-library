Traducción

Dinocrates the architect, trusting in his ideas and ingenuity, left Macedonia for the army while Alexander was in power, eager to win the king’s favor. He brought letters from relatives and friends at home to the leading officers and courtiers, hoping to gain easier access. They received him kindly, and he asked to be brought before Alexander as soon as possible. Although they promised to do so, they delayed while waiting for a suitable occasion. Thinking that they were making a fool of him, Dinocrates sought a resource within himself. He was very tall, with a pleasing face and an impressive, dignified physique. Trusting in these natural gifts, he left his clothes at the inn, anointed his body with oil, crowned his head with poplar leaves, draped a lion’s skin over his left shoulder and, holding a club in his right hand, advanced towards the tribunal where the king was administering justice.

Traducción

Bricks should be molded in spring or autumn so that they dry evenly. Those made at midsummer are defective because the sun rapidly bakes their outer skin, making it appear dry while the interior remains moist. As the interior later contracts in drying, it breaks through the dry surface, leaving the bricks cracked and weak. Bricks are most useful if molded two years before use, since they cannot dry thoroughly sooner. If fresh, undried bricks are built into a wall and coated with plaster that then sets hard, the bricks continue to settle and cannot retain the plaster’s height. Their shrinkage separates them from it; the plaster, detached from the masonry and too thin to support itself, breaks, while the settling walls are also damaged. For this reason the people of Utica use bricks in walls only after they have dried for five years and have been approved by a magistrate’s inspection.

Timber that lasts only a short time out of the ground can thus endure for a long time when buried in moisture.

The first foothills of the Apennines rise from the Tyrrhenian Sea between the Alps and the outer regions of Etruria. The ridge curves through Italy, approaches the Adriatic coast in its central bend and continues towards the strait. Trees growing there, nourished by moisture, become very large, and their veins swell with abundant sap. Once felled and hewn, they lose their vital force; as the veins stiffen and dry, their porous substance becomes weak and insubstantial. Such timber therefore lacks durability in buildings.
